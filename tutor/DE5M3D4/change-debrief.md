## Evaluate New Change Request ~ Report Back

*Each group gets about two minutes to explain:*

- what changed
- how the current design helps
- what the next team should do

**Your questions can probe their judgement:**

- Why does that belong in silver rather than gold?
- What happens if the new source is late or unavailable?
- Which existing output could be affected?
- What information would you need from the stakeholder before building?
- Would you extend the current pipeline or create a separate one?
