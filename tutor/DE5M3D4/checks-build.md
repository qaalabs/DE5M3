## Checks Build

Learners open `day4/checks_practice.ipynb` and work through four parts:

- Part 1: the five Day 3 assertions - run and pass (recap/reconnect)
- Part 2: a bad row is added, bare assert fails - they see the traceback message and the "stops here" point
- Part 3: try/except demo on the same bad data - all three checks run, one failure collected
- Part 4: they rewrite the five checks using try/except and add three of their own

The try/except pattern is new here. Give the group a couple of minutes on Part 3 before moving on - it is the hinge of the session.
