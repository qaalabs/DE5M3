## 🎥 Stakeholder


How to explain trust and caveats in plain language without disappearing into engineering detail

- Ask the group what a stakeholder cares about that an engineer doesn't, before showing the table
- Bridge to Prepare Your Explanation: they'll explain `gold_revenue` to the HomeSphere Head of Sales next
