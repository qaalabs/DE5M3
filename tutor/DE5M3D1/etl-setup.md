## Introduce the breakout activity

- Frame it: they've spent all day hand-coding ETL in Python - now look at how the industry does it with tools
- Split into 4 groups (A-D), one category each: Enterprise Commercial, Cloud-Managed, Open Source, Self-Service
- Small cohort, only 3 groups? Drop Self-Service (D) - the other three cover more ground
- Within each group, each person picks a different product from the list to investigate
- Point to the Investigation Framework in the doc - what it is, strengths/weaknesses, cost, use cases, fit for their org
- Report-back next: each person feeds back on their product, not just their group's category
