## S6 Learning Reflection

**Reconnect to the KSB (2 min)**

- Display S6. Ask learners to identify its actions: clean, validate and describe
- Unpack "systematically": use an ordered process and check its effects. "At all stages of ETL" is the wider scope; this activity contributes to it

**Everyone writes (4 min)**

- Use the three learner-page prompts: one cleaning decision and its downstream significance, the check and what its result showed, then how these relate to S6
- Keep this quiet and individual. Learners can extend Day 1 notes; retain a short snippet or actual result before the environment is cleared, and acknowledge guidance used

**Check and revise (4 min)**

- Learners check whether their outputs support their explanations and revise their notes. A lower row count alone does not show that the intended rows were removed
- For assessment for learning, review a few individual explanations during the task, with learners' agreement, and offer targeted feedback; learners mark unresolved gaps. No required chat or spoken report
- Check for the misconception that printing a count automatically validates data or stops incorrect output. Optional peer explanation can help resolve a gap after individual writing
