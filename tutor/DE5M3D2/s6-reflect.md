## S6 Learning Reflection

- Show S6 and ask learners to name its actions: clean, validate, describe. What does "systematically" add?
- Learners write individually in their notes using the three prompts on the page - quiet, no chat or report-back
- Remind them to keep a snippet or real result before the Fabric environment is cleared
- Misconception to listen for: a lower row count or printed count does not prove the right rows were removed
- Review a few explanations during the task and give targeted feedback; learners mark any gaps
