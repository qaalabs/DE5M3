## Design Review: Test the Reasoning

- Within the existing report-back slot, each group contributes one explanation: observed issue, design limitation, plausible consequence, proposed improvement and remaining limitation or trade-off
- Invite another group to test one link in that reasoning. Use code or recorded results where available; distinguish assumptions from observations and leave unresolved questions visible
- Build a concise visible set of justified design debts and K13 implications, correcting claims that raw and cleaned data share one folder or that no reusable cleaned Sales table exists
- Learners revise their own existing notes with a correction or useful insight. For assessment for learning, check whether the consequence and proposed response follow from the observation; offer targeted feedback
- Carry the debts into medallion mapping: which could clearer layers or a reusable Products table address, and which require other measures? Do not present medallion as a general cure for silent failures
