## Bridge to Day 4

**10 minutes. Low energy is fine -- keep it conversational, not a lecture.**

---

### Land the day

Draw the arc out loud:

> "This morning you named what was wrong. This afternoon you fixed the structure. You now have a pipeline with three clearly separated layers, built in Fabric, that produces the same answer as Day 1 -- but you could explain where every piece of data came from and why."

Pause. Let that sit.

---

### But is it enough?

Pose this question -- don't answer it:

> "If your line manager asked you tomorrow morning: 'Can I trust these numbers?' -- what would you show them?"

Take 2-3 answers. Common responses:
- "The gold table" -- good, but how do they know gold is correct?
- "The notebooks" -- can they read a notebook?
- "I'd just tell them" -- what if you're not there?

The point is not to make them feel stuck -- it's to surface that a well-structured pipeline is not the same as a trustworthy, explainable, handover-ready one.

---

### Set up Day 4

> "Tomorrow is about the gap between a pipeline that works and a pipeline that someone else can trust, run, and take over. That means validation -- checks that tell you when something is wrong before it reaches the gold layer. It means documentation. And it means being able to stand in front of a stakeholder and explain what you built and why they should rely on it."

---

### Close

> "You have the structure. Tomorrow you make it defensible."

Keep it short. Send them home with the question, not the answer.
