- Day overview and learning objectives

10:40-11:00 ~ Break (20 mins)

12:10-13:20 ~ Lunch (70 mins)

13:20-14:30 ~ Session 3 (70 mins)

14:30-14:50 ~ Break (20 mins)

14:50-15:50 ~ Session 4 (60 mins)

15:50-16:00 ~ Wrap-up (10 mins)
- Daily wrap
