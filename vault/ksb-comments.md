## The one that still looks light

The lightest one is probably:

K18 / S15 - streaming, batching, and on-demand services to move data from one location to another and optimise ingestion

At the moment, your module definitely covers batch-style movement and some cloud movement thinking, but streaming and on-demand are mostly implied rather than truly experienced. That is not fatal, but it is the clearest thin area if you are checking full coverage honestly. The same is true in practice for IoT and Marketing - they help you point to the next maturity steps, but they are not fully developed in the live design.

So if you wanted one small strengthening move, it would be:

make batch explicit in Day 1 and Day 2
use IoT or Marketing on Day 4 as the contrast case for streaming or on-demand
explicitly ask: “Which ingestion pattern does our current pipeline represent, and what would change if this were streaming or on-demand?”

That would strengthen K18/S15 without breaking the course.
