# Programme  Module Mapping

---
## Module 1 - Fundamentals of Data Engineering

**Purpose:** context and foundations

Introduces the role, the landscape, core concepts, data types, sources, modelling, quality, governance, and the wider meaning of data engineering. It helps learners understand the field before they start building within it.

---
### Module 2 - Data Storage and Management

**Purpose:** where data lives

Focuses on databases, NoSQL, distributed systems, warehousing, lakes/lakehouses, and storage choices. This is the storage undercurrent in the lifecycle.

---
### Module 3 - Data Processing, Transformation & ETL

**Purpose:** the middle of the lifecycle - how data moves and is transformed

This is where learners take existing source data, ingest it, clean it, integrate it, transform it, and prepare it for trustworthy use. It deliberately owns the middle of the lifecycle.

---
### Module 4 - Planning a Data Engineering Product

**Purpose:** designing towards serving

Moves from data movement into product thinking - requirements, design, governance, sustainability, risk, documentation, tooling, and communication. It is about planning what will eventually serve users and the organisation.

---
### Module 5 - Data Engineering Product Development

**Purpose:** building towards serving

Turns planning into implementation: product construction, testing, technical documentation, collaboration, prototype evaluation, quality tracking, risk handling, and continuous improvement.

---
### Module 6 - Data Operations

**Purpose:** operating what serves consumers

Covers monitoring, troubleshooting, deployment, incident response, business continuity, root cause analysis, quality assurance, and continuous improvement. This is the operational lifecycle once data products are in use.

---
### Module 7 - Emerging Technologies and Personal Development

**Purpose:** future directions across the lifecycle

Looks ahead to new technologies, AI, ML-related themes, and personal strategies for staying current as the field evolves.

---
## Why this is strong

It gives the programme a believable story:

* first understand the field
* then understand storage
* then understand movement and transformation
* then plan products
* then build products
* then operate products
* then look forward

## So I would summarise the whole programme like this

**Programme lifecycle map**

* Module 1 - foundations and context
* Module 2 - storage and data platform choices
* Module 3 - ingestion, processing, and transformation
* Module 4 - planning data products
* Module 5 - building data products
* Module 6 - operating and improving data products
* Module 7 - emerging technologies and future development
