# Laurillard-learning-types

## acquisition
Learning through acquisition is when the teacher controls the learning narrative and learners are passively:

- listening to a lecture or podcast;
- reading from books or websites; or 
- watching demos or videos.

## production
Learning through production is the way the teacher motivates the learner to consolidate what they have learned by articulating their current conceptual understanding and how they used it in practice.

## practice
Learning through practice enables the learner to adapt their actions to the task goal, and use the feedback to improve their next action. 

Feedback may come from self-reflection, from peers, from the teacher, or from the activity itself, if it shows them how to improve the result of their action in relation to the goal.

## investigation
Learning through investigation guides the learner to explore, compare and critique the texts, documents and resources that reflect the concepts and ideas being taught.

## discussion
Learning through discussion requires the learner to articulate their ideas and questions, and to challenge and respond to the ideas and questions from the teacher and/or from their peers.

## collaboration	
- Students work together on a project where they have to produce a shared output. 
- Collaboration is about the process of working together. 
- Students negotiate their ideas and practice until they agree. 
- They challenge each other and provide peer feedback to develop the best output they can.
