# DE5 Module 3 Apply (after live)

Your workplace activity brief for ​Data Processing, Transformation, and ETL

As mentioned in the previous step, your first task in the Apply stage is to prepare for your workplace activity, where you can apply your new knowledge and skills.

This step details your workplace activity brief for this module.

In this workplace activity, you are tasked with completing the following activities whilst documenting them in a report. The entire report should include insights and findings from each task you complete along with a comprehensive overview of data processing, transformation, and ETL practices within your organisation. 

Throughout your report, demonstrate how you used initiative and innovation to problem-solve and troubleshoot, providing creative solutions to the tasks you completed. Highlight specific instances where your approach was particularly innovative or effective. (B5)

To effectively accomplish this activity, complete the steps/tasks below.

---

## Data processing (K11, S7, S8) 

1. Compare and contrast at least three cloud computing platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and describe scenarios where each would be most beneficial in your organisation. 

2. Compare different types of data stores you have used in your organisation (e.g., SQL, NoSQL) and describe how you optimised their performance.  

3. Develop an algorithm to extract data from different sources. Evaluate the success of the algorithm and document the process based on your organisational data ingestion framework. 

4. Provide a case study from your workplace of an incident in a data pipeline, detailing the root cause analysis, troubleshooting steps, and resolution provided to stakeholders. 

5. Write a report on how you adhere to your organisation's data privacy policies and security standards in your daily work. 

---

## Data transformation

1. Create a step-by-step guide on deploying a new data pipeline using a chosen data integration platform in your organisation. Include considerations for security, scalability, and governance. 

2. Develop and document a simple automated data pipeline using a programming language and a graphical data integration platform. Highlight the security, scalability, and governance measures implemented. 

3. Write a script to query and manipulate a dataset. Implement automated validation checks and document the methodologies used to move data between systems.

4. Use the guide and other documentation to create an ETL/ELT pipeline with emphasis on the transformation part (Pandas).

---

## ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) (K18, S15) 

1. Take a dataset in your job, perform ETL processes, and document the steps taken to clean and validate the data. Show how combining disparate data sources delivers value to your organisation. 

2. Set up and document a data ingestion process using streaming, batching, and on-demand services. Optimise the process and explain the choices made. 

3. List the data engineering tools you use, describe their purposes, and provide examples of how you apply them in your work. 

---

## Timescales

We expect this Apply stage to take approximately ​24 hours​ to complete, spread across the remaining duration of this module.
