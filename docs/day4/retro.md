# Facilitator Notes - Module Retrospective

*Trainer-only. Closes Day 4 and the module.*

---

## Make the journey visible

Put the four-day arc on screen or say it clearly:

> "Day 1 - you had a messy CSV and a question. You made the logic work.
> Day 2 - you moved it into the cloud. Same logic, new environment.
> Day 3 - you gave it structure. Same output, better architecture.
> Day 4 - you made it supportable. Same pipeline, now someone else can own it."

The business output has not changed since Day 1. What changed is how reliably 
and legibly it produces that output, and how much of you it requires to keep running.

---

## Ask the group

**"What surprised you this week?"**

Do not prompt this one. Let it land and wait. The first answer usually opens 
the conversation genuinely.

**"What will you look at differently at work on Monday?"**

Things to draw out if the conversation stalls:

- A pipeline they inherited that has no documentation
- A process that has no validation - just runs and produces output
- A handover they have given or received that missed something important
- A stakeholder who never quite trusted the numbers - and why that might be

**"What question do you still have?"**

This is an honest close. Not everything is answered. Name that directly:

> "This module is a foundation, not a complete picture. What are you still unsure about?"

Take two or three questions. Answer briefly where you can. Where you cannot, 
name which module picks it up or acknowledge it is genuinely hard.

---

## Closing words

> "Four days ago you had a messy CSV and a question.
> Today you have a validated, layered pipeline running in the cloud, with 
> documentation and checks, that you can explain to an engineer or a stakeholder.
> That is not a small thing."

---

## Hand over to evaluation

Move directly to the module evaluation.

Remind learners to be specific in their feedback - what worked, what could be 
clearer, what they would have wanted more or less of.

If there is time, ask: "What one thing would you tell next week's cohort to focus on?"
