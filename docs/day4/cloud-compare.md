!!! question "If HomeSphere had to pick one cloud platform for its ETL pipeline, which would you recommend - and why?"

## The scenario

HomeSphere wants to move off the manual, hand-built pipeline you have been running all week and onto a fully managed cloud platform. Leadership has asked for a short pitch comparing the three major providers before they commit.

Your group will be assigned **one platform**: AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.

---

| **Biggest strength** for a scenario like HomeSphere | |
| **Biggest limitation or risk** | |

You do not need to become an expert in the platform - a confident best guess from vendor docs or product pages is fine.

---

## Report Back

*Pitch your platform to the class:*

- Which services would carry HomeSphere's pipeline, end to end?
- What is the strongest reason to choose this platform?
- What would make you hesitate to recommend it?

After all three pitches, as a class: **which platform actually fits HomeSphere best, and why did the other two lose?**
