# Facilitator Notes - Respond to Change Debrief

*Trainer-only. After the group design task, before Wrap.*

---

## How to run it

One rep per group, one minute each. Ask for three things only:

- Which change request they had
- Where it enters the pipeline (bronze, silver, or gold)
- The single biggest risk or unknown they could not resolve

Do not let groups walk through all seven questions out loud - the page or diagram speaks for itself. Hold it up or share the screen while they talk.

---

## What to listen for

Good answers reuse the week's vocabulary without being prompted:

- Bronze as a landing point that does not force a rebuild - the new feed lands, existing layers are untouched until proven ready
- Validation as the thing that would catch a schema mismatch (Group D) or a missing notification (Group C) rather than a person noticing late
- Documentation as what lets someone else pick this up - the handover artefact from this morning is exactly what the next team would need
- Reuse - most groups should find that silver and gold logic barely changes; the work is mostly at the edges

Redirect groups that treat this as a from-scratch design: "What from this week would you keep exactly as it is?"

---

## Key point to land

> "Maturity is not being finished. It is being able to absorb change."

Connect it back to the open of the day: safe to run, easy to inherit, easy to explain - and now, able to cope with change. All four are what "maturity" actually means for a pipeline. None of them mean "perfect" or "done."

---

## Bridge to Wrap

> "You didn't build today's requirement - you didn't need to. You already know where it goes, what it touches, and what it risks. That's the difference four days has made."

Move directly into the Day 4 wrap.
