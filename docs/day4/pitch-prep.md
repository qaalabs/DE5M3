# Prepare Your Stakeholder Explanation

*Breakout - Session 3. Pairs or small groups.*
*Aim: 20 minutes.*

---

## The scenario

> You are presenting to the HomeSphere Head of Sales.
> She does not care about notebooks, bronze layers, or Delta tables.
> She wants to know what this data product is, whether she can trust it, and what it tells her about the business.

---

## Your task

Prepare a short explanation - two to three minutes - that covers:

1. **What `gold_revenue` is**
   What does it contain? What question does it answer?

2. **Why it can be trusted**
   What gives you confidence in the numbers? You should explain this without using the words bronze, silver, gold, medallion, or assertion.

3. **What caveats remain**
   What does it not tell her? What could still be wrong? What assumptions are built in?

4. **What she can do with it**
   A concrete next action or decision the data supports.

---

## Prompts

- What would she care about that you have not thought about from an engineering perspective?
- What would make her sceptical?
- What would make her trust the number she sees?
- What would make her come back and say the data is wrong?

---

## What to avoid

- Disappearing into engineering detail - she does not need to know the pipeline has two notebooks
- Vague reassurance - "it's been cleaned and validated" means nothing without specifics
- Overclaiming - if you do not know what the data covers, say so

---

## Structure your explanation

Use this as a rough outline if it helps:

> "This report shows HomeSphere's revenue broken down by product category, based on sales data from [source].
> The figures are reliable because [one or two specific reasons].
> There are a few things to be aware of: [caveats].
> What this supports is [decision or action]."

You do not have to follow this exactly - but your explanation should cover the same ground.

---

## Prepare to share

You will present to the room after the breakout. Keep it to two or three minutes.
Decide who will speak - or split it between two people.
