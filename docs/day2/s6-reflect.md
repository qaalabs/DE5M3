Yesterday you cleaned the Sales Orders data locally. Today you did the same in Fabric, in [Lab 2.3 - Clean the sales data](../labs/23-cloud-clean.md).

Read S6 and identify its actions: **clean**, **validate** and **describe**. What does **systematically** add? Notice its scope: **at all stages of ETL**. Today's cleaning activity helps you practise part of that wider skill.

## Write it down

Open your learning notes and record one brief example using the prompts below. A short paragraph or a few bullets is enough:

1. **Clean:** Choose one cleaning step you completed. Why was it appropriate for this data, and what could go wrong downstream if you omitted it?
2. **Validate and describe:** What check did you run, and what did its result tell you about the data?
3. **Connect to S6:** Use your explanation to show how cleaning, validating and describing work together in S6. Note anything you still need to understand or practise.

Work on your own first. Use your actual results and say where you used guidance or a solution. Add a short code snippet or result to make your notes useful when you revisit them. Keep it in your personal notes before the Fabric environment is cleared.

You can build on your Day 1 notes rather than repeat the full cleaning process.

## Check your understanding

Check that your result supports your explanation: a lower row count alone does not show that the right rows were removed. Use your code and outputs to revise your note or mark a gap for feedback.
