# Activity: Choosing the Right Data Architecture

## Scenario

You are a data engineering team consulting for HomeSphere, a smart home 
technology company.

HomeSphere has:

- Sales transactions updated daily
- A product catalogue with nested specifications
- A need for daily revenue reporting by category and region

Your task is to **decide why your allocated architecture is the best fit for 
HomeSphere, and justify your choice**.

---

## Your Architecture

Your group will be assigned 1 of the following:

- **Data Warehouse**
- **Data Lake**
- **Data Lakehouse**

## Your Task

Work as a group to prepare a short response.

You will present your answer back to the class.

---

## Questions to help you:

### 1. What problem does your architecture solve?

Focus on the **core pain point**, not just a definition.

### 2. Where does ETL or ELT fit?

Think about:

- Do you transform data **before** loading?
- Or load raw data and transform later?

### 3. Why is your architecture the BEST choice for this scenario?

Be specific to HomeSphere.

### 4. What would go wrong if this was the WRONG choice?

Think about:

- Performance
- Cost
- Complexity
- Data usability

### 5. One strength and one weakness

Be balanced - no solution is perfect.

---

## Output (keep it simple)

Be ready to explain:

- Why your architecture works
- Where it would fail

No slides needed - just clear thinking.
